Coach Learning

Colour-blind players need equipment instructions that work at a glance. Grey scale help removes colour names and shows contrast. If two colours look similar in grey scale, they may be confusing during fast training.

Best equipment colours

Use strong contrast: yellow vs blue, white vs black, black vs yellow, white markers on dark cones, blue bibs vs yellow bibs.

Risky equipment colours

Red vs green, orange vs green grass, yellow vs light green, blue vs purple, white vs light grey, green bibs on grass and black vs navy.

Why grey scale helps

Grey scale shows light-vs-dark contrast without colour names. If equipment blends in grey scale, it may blend during movement, shade, rain, floodlights or bright sun.

Why it affects training

The player may run to the wrong cone, miss a target gate, join the wrong bib team or react late because the colour instruction is hard to decode.
